## 1. Executive Concept & System Architecture

The Ram Mandir Wedding Invitation is a luxury, mobile-first, vertical storybook wedding invitation originally built on Framer. It uses a **multi-tier depth illusion (2.5D Parallax & WebGL Shaders)** combined with an **ultra-long continuous parchment scroll**.

### The Stacking Layers (Z-Index Hierarchy)
1. **Background Layer (`z-index: 1`) — Interactive WebGL Sky Canvas:**
   - Real-time sine-wave mathematical cloth simulation (the orange "Jai Shri Ram" triangular dhwaja flag).
   - Craig Reynolds' Boids flocking algorithm (flock of 3D birds flying continuously in natural arcs).
   - Sun ray / particle dust shader drifting across a warm gradient morning sky.
2. **Foreground Hero Layer (`z-index: 10`) — Temple & Entrance Aisle:**
   - File: `lKVzZvE9zjHal1zLmfO4koCjo.webp` (1515 × 3526 px).
   - Alpha-channel cutout: The sky behind the temple spire (shikhar) is **100% transparent**. This allows the birds and waving flag to render *behind* the temple towers while the temple sits *in front*.
   - Parallax effect: Moves at `transform: translateY(...)` slightly slower than scroll speed to create a 3D depth effect.
3. **Interactive UI Overlay Layer (`z-index: 20`) — Dynamic Content:**
   - Couple Introduction Text ("Akash weds Drashti").
   - Live JavaScript Countdown Clock (`Days : Hours : Mins : Secs`).
   - Background Music Audio Controller (Mute / Unmute floating pill).
4. **Ceremony Timeline Scroll (`z-index: 5`) — Continuous Seamless Canvas:**
   - File: `HRENeallVVOHG1sTSUCePuUH4.webp` (1515 × 9148 px).
   - One uninterrupted golden-bordered parchment that flows from the temple aisle down through all 6 wedding ceremonies to the footer.
5. **Social Metadata Layer (Out-of-DOM):**
   - File: `3JekLBRwVWegsCm3gX3wOcYeM.webp` (1200 × 630 px).
   - Landscape 16:9 card containing baked-in couple names, temple, and toran borders for WhatsApp/iMessage/Instagram previews.

## 1. Concept & Architectural DNA

The **Mahabodhi Buddhist Wedding Invitation** adapts the luxury vertical storybook architecture of the Pehlakhat template to the serene spiritual elegance of a Vedic-Buddhist wedding union.

### The Sacred Stacking Layers (Z-Index Hierarchy)
1. **Background Layer (`z-index: 1`) — Interactive Sky & Bodhi Leaf Canvas:**
   - Real-time physics particle simulation: Translucent golden Bodhi leaves (*Ficus religiosa*) and soft pink/white lotus petals drifting continuously across the morning sky.
   - Flocking white cranes / doves gliding past the ancient stepped pyramidal Mahabodhi spire.
   - Ambient golden sunrise radiance shader with warm morning mist.
2. **Foreground Hero Layer (`z-index: 10`) — Mahabodhi Temple Sanctum & Lotus Walkway:**
   - **Resolution:** `1515 × 3526 px` (Aspect Ratio ~1:2.33)
   - **Sky Cutout:** Upper 30% background sky has a clean alpha channel (100% transparent), allowing the animated flocking cranes and drifting Bodhi leaves to pass seamlessly behind the stone spire.
   - **Couple Walkway:** Bride in crimson & gold brocade silk saree with jasmine garlands, and groom in ivory silk kurta with saffron embroidered angavastram walking up the ancient stone steps toward the sacred inner sanctum.
   - **Countdown Arch:** Symmetrical carved stone railing (*vedika*) and golden lotus urns framing a negative negative space archway for the live countdown clock.
3. **Interactive UI Overlay Layer (`z-index: 20`) — Dynamic Features:**
   - Auspicious Pali Inscription: *“नमो तस्स भगवतो अरहतो सम्मासम्बुद्धस्स”* (*Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Samma Sambuddhassa*).
   - Couple Typography: "Siddharth weds Maya".
   - Live JavaScript Countdown Clock (`Days : Hours : Mins : Secs`).
   - Floating Buddhist Lotus Audio Pill: Plays bansuri flute & Tibetan singing bowl resonance (`audio/buddhist_flute_blessing.mp3`).
4. **Ceremony Timeline Canvas (`z-index: 5`):**
   - Seamless continuous scroll hosting the 6 auspicious ceremonies:
     1. **Mangala Vandana & Chanting** (Paritta blessings for auspicious peace)
     2. **Suprabhatam & Haldi Snan** (Sacred saffron & lotus petal spring-water blessing)
     3. **Bodhi Sangeet & Cultural Twilight** (Bansuri flute, sitar, and joy under lantern-lit trees)
     4. **Sacred Bodhi Vivaha & Sutra-Bandhan** (Holy marriage vows, sacred thread binding & holy water libation)
     5. **Pradakshina of Thousand Lamps** (Candlelit circumambulation with golden lotus butter lamps)
     6. **Grand Bodhi Gala Reception** (Royal banquet and celebration of eternal union)
